      bloop is a small, Rust-inspired language compiled through LLVM. This page runs
      a WebAssembly build of its interpreter directly in your browser -- nothing is sent to a
      server. Try it out in the REPL tab, or see how a source file lowers to
      BLIR (Bloop's own intermediate representation) in the Compiler tab.
    

    

      Source, architecture notes, and more background are on
      GitHub.
